# Inclusion and exclusion criteria

Eligibility criteria applied during the systematic screening process to determine which publications were included in the review. The same criteria were applied at title and abstract screening and at full-text screening. A publication was included only if it met all four inclusion criteria and none of the exclusion criteria.

## Inclusion criteria

1. The publication is in English.
2. The research focuses on anomaly detection in marine ecology, specifically detecting anomalies in:
   - (a) Organism behaviour or movement patterns
   - (b) Biological or ecological events
   - (c) Biological or ecological data quality
3. The research objectives are explicitly stated, and the statistical analysis is suitable for addressing the study's research questions and objectives.
4. Anomaly detection methods are applied and evaluated, with the experimental design outlined and results presented.

## Exclusion criteria

1. The article is not written in English.
2. The focus is not on biological or ecological anomalies in marine systems (for example, studies focused solely on physical habitat characterisation, geophysical features, or maritime operations without ecological context).
3. Anomaly detection methods are not applied or evaluated.
4. The research objectives are not clearly defined.
5. The research does not include a defined methodology for anomaly detection, or lacks sufficient detail in its experimental design.
6. The statistical analysis and conclusions are not robust or reproducible.

## Where the decisions are recorded

The decision for each record, with the reason for each exclusion, is in `data/02_screening/screening_deduplicated.xlsx`.
